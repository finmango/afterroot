AfterRoot is built by FinMango, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit focused on financial health. We've reached 1M+ people across 13+ countries. Our existing tools — a Food Assistance Calculator and Food Desert Analyzer — are live now.

AfterRoot applies the same approach to disaster recovery: cross-referencing fragmented programs, surfacing what people qualify for, and guiding them through applications. Different problem, same muscle.
